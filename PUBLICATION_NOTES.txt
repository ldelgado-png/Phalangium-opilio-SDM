Phalangium-opilio-SDM: peer-review archive, 25 October 2026.

Journal: Biotropica. The submitted manuscript title is recorded in README.md and CITATION.cff.

The manuscript analysis uses the fixed western Palaearctic operational rectangle, LQ/RM2, cloglog output and clamping. The archived scientific rasters and figure exports are unchanged; data/review_artifact_manifest.csv records their Git blob identifiers and sizes.

Public review starts with docs/REVIEWER_GUIDE.md and Rscript run_all.R. Rscript run_all.R --with-rasters regenerates the derived tables from public inputs. The driver stops on failure and writes a local execution log.

Coordinate-bearing Venezuelan event files are excluded from the current branch. R26/R27 location extraction and overlays require the separately supplied field input. The public three-cell summary supports review without those coordinates. Local outputs are ignored by Git.

Rebuilding the fitted model requires the separately obtained GBIF archive and WorldClim predictors. A successful archive check does not certify full model refitting. R29 is an optional sensitivity workflow, not a deposited new result.

Historical filenames are retained for input compatibility. They do not denote another model, a change in journal, or outstanding model selection.
