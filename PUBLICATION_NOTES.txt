Phalangium-opilio-SDM: repository and reproducibility notes.

- The manuscript uses the fixed western Palaearctic operational calibration rectangle, the LQ/RM2 model, and the clamped South American projection.
- Historical filenames containing "pilot", "EXPLORATORY", "NOT_FINAL" or "figura2" are retained for provenance and path compatibility; the manuscript figure is Figure 3.
- The published repository now includes the seven-event coordinate-bearing Venezuelan transfer table at results/26_venezuela_site_transfer_values.csv, in addition to the three-cell coordinate-free summary. These field coordinates should be considered publicly disclosed; confirm that their release is consistent with the authors' specimen-data and locality-sharing policy.
- Source rasters, final figure exports, model-evaluation summaries and R26/R27 are deposited. The additional R28 and R29 scripts are provided as supplementary workflows; their existence does not establish that they have been executed or that their outputs support new manuscript claims.
- Raw GBIF and WorldClim source archives are retrieved separately using their documented sources and checksums.
- run_all.R is an unverified convenience driver, not a demonstrated clean-checkout reproduction test. It continues after individual step failures; inspect results/run_all_log.csv and confirm all required stages succeeded before asserting end-to-end reproducibility.
