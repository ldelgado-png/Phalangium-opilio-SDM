Phalangium-opilio-SDM: publication notes.

- Historical filenames containing "pilot", "EXPLORATORY", "NOT_FINAL" or "figura2" are kept for path
  compatibility with the R scripts. They do not denote a different or pending model.
- The deposited rasters and figures were force-added to Git although .gitignore lists *.tif and *.pdf.
- Venezuelan site coordinates are public: results/26_venezuela_site_transfer_values.csv (and Table 1
  of the manuscript). With the deposited rasters, R/26 and the site overlay of R/27 can be re-run.
- Raw GBIF download and WorldClim rasters are not redistributed: obtain the GBIF archive by DOI
  (SHA-256 in data/README.md) and WorldClim 2.1 from worldclim.org. See run_all.R for the order.
- Statements of end-to-end reproduction should be made only after run_all.R has been executed on a clean
  checkout and its session log (results/sessionInfo.txt) has been committed.
